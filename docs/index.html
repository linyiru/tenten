A variable CJK dot font. Every glyph is drawn as dots on a 12 × 12 lattice, and the dots are what vary: wght sets their size, ROND their shape, from square pixels to round dots. Four fonts, one per locale, each built from its own face of Fusion Pixel Font so every character has its local form.
